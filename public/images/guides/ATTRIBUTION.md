# Guide photography

Checked 4 November 2026. All photographs below are published under the standard [Unsplash License](https://unsplash.com/license). They are illustrative: pictured people and projects are not presented as WildTeam staff, learners or projects. Visible credits link to the source photo. The photo catalogue is used for individual guide heroes and Article image metadata, overriding legacy illustrations in published content without changing reviewed prose or data. The hub uses the original user-supplied sector illustrations. Hero selections avoid campaign branding and favour neutral nature, habitat and wildlife-related subjects where relevant.
